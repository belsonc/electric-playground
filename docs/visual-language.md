**Visual Language**

- the player should be able to see electricity moving through the circuit
- electricity is represented as finite units.  periods, or dashes, or similar - but every ____ should represent a "chunk" of electricity.
- you should be able to watch the flow of electricity through the circuit
- if the electricity comes to a resistor, there shouldn't be a backup - it should come into the resistor at normal speed, and leave the resistor at a slower flow.  example - the electricity enters the resistor at a rate of 1 per 1 second, and leaves at a rate of 1 per 5 seconds.  there shouldn't be any sort of visual disruption to the flow of electricity, just the rate that it's "sent"
- LEDs should be appropriately bright relative to the electricity they receive.  an led with a 220 resistor should be brighter than one with a 10k resistor
