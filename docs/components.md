 # Components

## Battery

A battery is a simple power source, similar in appearance to a 9-volt battery. Electricity should visibly flow from one pole, through the circuit, and back into the other pole.

## Wire

A wire is a simple straight line connecting a component to the nearest connection point on another component—for example, a resistor post to an LED post. Wires should avoid covering components whenever possible and should clearly communicate their connections. Render them with the visual weight of a thick underline or cell border.

## Resistor

A resistor is a box with a post on each side, resembling a box-and-whisker chart. Its purpose is to restrict the amount of electricity passing through it. A stronger resistor (higher value) allows less electricity through; a weaker resistor (lower value) allows more.

## LED

An LED should resemble a real LED, with medium-to-long posts connected at the bottom. It should illuminate proportionally to the electricity it receives: less electricity produces a dimmer light, while more produces a brighter light. If the incoming electricity is excessive, the LED should break accordingly.
